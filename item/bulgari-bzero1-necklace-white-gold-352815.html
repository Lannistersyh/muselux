Design notes

B.zero1 rewards a view from the side. Suspended on a fine chain, its compact round pendant carries a sense of depth that changes as it turns. The white-gold version makes those edges and shadows the central event, with the lettering around the rim adding a graphic border. It is a useful study in how a necklace can feel architectural without becoming large. Give it a quiet background and let the small volume do the work, especially when the rest of an outfit favors clean lines.

Three details to notice

  • A small round pendant in 18K white gold.
  • The B.zero1 layered profile and BVLGARI lettering frame an open center.
  • Approximately 1.5 cm pendant height; chain positions at 38, 41.5 and 45 cm.

How to wear it

Set the pendant against a smooth dark knit to make the white metal legible. Try the available chain positions with your neckline before adding any other necklace or collar detail.
