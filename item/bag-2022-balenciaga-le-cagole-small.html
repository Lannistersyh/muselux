FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Le Cagole builds its character through an accumulation of small gestures. Textured black leather, knotted zipper pulls and aged silver hardware sit around a soft crescent shape, while the heart mirror adds a deliberately sweet interruption. The result has movement even when the bag is still: straps, laces and curves pull the eye across its surface. It brings a lived-in mood to clean clothing and can also hold its own among stronger textures. The small size keeps that decorative density concentrated and visually energetic.

Three details to notice

  • Arena lambskin with aged-silver hardware
  • Adjustable strap with a braided leather shoulder pad
  • Removable heart mirror and zipped pockets

How to wear it

Pair it with a grey knit, dark straight jeans and a long coat for a relaxed balance. Keep jewellery restrained so the mirror, knots and hardware can supply the detail.
