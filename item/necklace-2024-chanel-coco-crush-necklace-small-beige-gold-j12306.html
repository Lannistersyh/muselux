FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Coco Crush translates the visual softness of quilting into a small, sculptural pendant. Across its rounded surface, crossing lines create a pattern that feels familiar without needing a large emblem. The BEIGE GOLD tone adds another point of interest: compare it with the metals already in your wardrobe and notice the difference in color. This is a necklace to approach through texture and proportion. A quiet neckline gives the compact form room to show how much character can live within a small amount of space.

Three details to notice

  • A small quilted-motif pendant in 18K BEIGE GOLD.
  • Crossing incisions and a rounded profile define the surface.
  • The snap-hook clasp works with four extension loops.

How to wear it

Try it with a soft ivory blouse or a plain black knit. Keep nearby hardware simple, and choose the chain position that leaves the quilted pendant clear of the fabric edge.
