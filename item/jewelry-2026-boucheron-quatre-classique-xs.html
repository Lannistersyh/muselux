FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

The smallest Quatre Classique ring turns contrast into a precise line around the finger. Warm gold, cool white metal, brown PVD and a fine diamond row each catch the light differently, so the effect changes as the hand moves. The compact proportions make its stacked architecture easier to introduce to an everyday jewelry wardrobe. It can bring a graphic edge to soft knitwear or echo the clean seams of a tailored sleeve, with the four textures providing detail without requiring a large stone at the center.

Three details to notice

  • Four characteristic Quatre textures in the XS format.
  • Yellow, white and pink 18K gold with brown PVD.
  • 43 round diamonds total approximately 0.13 carat; final specifications may vary.

How to wear it

Let it sit on its own beside a clean cuff, or pair it with one slim plain band. Cream knitwear and dark denim make the brown and gold contrasts especially clear.
