FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

The Force 10 Rise pendant compresses a cable-and-buckle idea into a small, clear shape at the neckline. Yellow gold brings a warm outline to the loop, while a narrow diamond accent draws attention to the central crossing. The chain leaves space around the motif so the design can be read without a large pendant or a heavy setting. Its compact proportions make it useful for a wardrobe built around clean collars and fine knits, adding a polished detail that can remain personal rather than dominating the entire outfit.

Three details to notice

  • 18K yellow gold with the Force 10 cable-and-buckle motif.
  • 39 brilliant-cut white diamonds total approximately 0.10 carat.
  • 45 cm chain with 39 and 42 cm adjustment points; this is the small pendant, not the 80 cm long necklace.

How to wear it

Set the chain at a length that sits clearly above a shirt opening, or wear it against a fine dark knit. A small gold hoop can echo the loop without crowding the neckline.
