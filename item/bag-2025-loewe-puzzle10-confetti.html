FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Confetti makes the Puzzle’s tenth anniversary feel like a celebration you can carry. Hand-embroidered pieces of leather cover the small calfskin bag with a densely colored surface, while its dark blue edges preserve the clarity of the geometric silhouette. The contrast is the pleasure: familiar folded lines underneath, a lively field of tiny details above. Created as the anniversary capsule’s new design, this edition offers an expressive view of LOEWE’s leather work and turns the Puzzle into a distinct occasion piece.

Three details to notice

  • Small Puzzle in classic calfskin with hand-embroidered leather confetti.
  • Officially described as a limited original edition for the tenth-anniversary capsule.
  • Exact multicolor reference A510S21XCD-9990.

How to wear it

Give the surface space with a monochrome outfit in navy, cream or black. A clean jacket and simple shoes make the bag’s color and texture feel deliberate for an evening out.
