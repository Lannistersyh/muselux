FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Dada uses a narrow belt to give soft black nappa leather a gently gathered outline. Long handles and small metal details keep the construction visually light, while the embossed Prada lettering stays close to the surface. The small format combines a magnetic closure with a leather-lined interior and center pocket, offering a considered balance between softness and order. Its appeal lies in the subtle way the belt changes the bag’s volume, making an otherwise minimal shape feel expressive without depending on a large logo.

Three details to notice

  • Nappa lambskin shaped by a narrow leather belt.
  • Magnetic closure and nappa leather lining with center pocket.
  • Small dimensions: 25 × 18.5 × 11.5 cm; handle drop 32 cm.

How to wear it

Use its gathered shape with a crisp shirt and a simple midi skirt, or tuck it beside a wool jacket and straight jeans. A restrained palette lets the leather folds remain visible.
