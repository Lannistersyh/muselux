FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A small Poké Ball changes the language of a gold-tone necklace. Rather than relying on a conventional jewel, Fendi’s collaboration uses a familiar game object as a compact sculptural pendant, with the house signature supplying a second layer of recognition. The contrast is the point: the smooth rounded form feels playful against the orderly links of the chain. Worn with a plain neckline, it brings a precise pop-culture reference to an otherwise restrained outfit, while the opening motif adds a small element of discovery.

Three details to notice

  • Gold-finished brass chain and Poké Ball motif.
  • The Poké Ball opens to reveal the Fendi FF signature.
  • Part of the collaboration released4January 2024.

How to wear it

Keep the pendant clear of a shirt placket or printed top. Plain black cotton makes the gold-tone circle easy to see.
