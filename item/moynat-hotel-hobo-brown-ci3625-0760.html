Design notes

The Hôtel Moynat Hobo turns the House’s travel heritage into an easy everyday silhouette. Colourful hotel-label motifs stand out against brown M canvas, while cowhide trims and contrasting stitching define the soft, generous shape. A top handle offers a relaxed shoulder carry; the removable, adjustable strap provides a crossbody option and finishes with signature M-shaped clasps. A zip closure keeps the opening neat. Part of the limited-edition Hôtel Moynat capsule, this version combines the visual character of vintage luggage with a practical format for a day in the city. Created with Storm Pablo and Marvin Douglas, this collaboration capsule has no production quantity stated in the cited sources.

Three details to notice

  • Brown M canvas with hotel-label motifs, contrasting stitching and the Moynat Crown leather patch
  • Top leather handle plus removable, adjustable strap with signature M-shaped clasps
  • Zip closure, cowhide leather trims and gold-colour hardware; made in Italy

How to wear it

Let the hotel-label colours enliven a plain trench, dark denim and loafers. Carry it by the short handle for a relaxed city look, then attach the long strap when you want your hands free.
