FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

These dragonflies hang beneath small pavé hoops, letting the wings move just below the ear. Pink and purple stones alternate across the pair, creating a gentle difference between the two sides of the face. A Crystal Pearl at each head brings a round, smooth note to the faceted surfaces. The scale makes the figurative motif easy to recognize while keeping the composition compact. Against a plain blouse or soft knit, the earrings supply colour, movement and a slightly whimsical focal point without requiring a matching necklace.

Three details to notice

  • Pastel dragonflies hang from pavé hoops.
  • Alternating pink and purple wings with a Crystal Pearl at each head.
  • Rhodium-plated metal; each earring measures approximately 1 × 3/4 inch.

How to wear it

Pull the hair into a low knot and pair the earrings with a pale blouse or charcoal knit. A bare neckline gives the moving wings space, while a small ring can finish the look.
