FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A single elongated gauge link gives each HardWear earring an immediate outline. The polished yellow gold turns as the head moves, creating broad reflections rather than the pinpoint sparkle of a gemstone. Medium proportions make the shape readable from a distance while leaving the neckline available for the cut of a shirt or jacket. Their graphic character suits a wardrobe of clean collars and uncomplicated fabrics. Small differences in hair, sleeve and shoulder proportions can shift them from a daily accent to the main focus of an evening look.

Three details to notice

  • 18K yellow gold with the HardWear gauge-link motif.
  • Medium motif measures 1.1 inches in length.
  • Official product family 73584531 groups several manufacturing SKUs.

How to wear it

Pull the hair back and choose a crisp collared shirt or a plain boatneck. A dark jacket frames the warm gold; a bare neckline makes the elongated links especially clear.
