FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

The Amazona 180 turns an opening into the design’s main event. Its single rounded handle holds the upper frame while the front panel falls into a shallow curve, revealing the contrasting interior. In black, the effect is architectural and supple: long zipper pulls extend the outline and a small logo keeps the focus on construction. The large size works particularly well as the main accessory in an understated outfit. It introduces movement and asymmetry to clean tailoring, especially when the rest of the look stays narrow and quiet.

Three details to notice

  • Soft calfskin with a single toron top handle.
  • Designed for an open carry, with a concealed closure securing the main compartment.
  • Removable shoulder and crossbody straps.

How to wear it

Use the large black version with a straight wool coat, a light shirt, and slim trousers. Carry it by the single handle to give the open silhouette and long pulls their full visual effect.
