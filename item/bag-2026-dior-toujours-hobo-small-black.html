FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

The small Toujours Hobo translates Dior’s geometric Cannage pattern into a rounded silhouette. The curved opening and pale gold CD Lock bring the eye toward the handle, while black lambskin keeps the quilted surface readable without a strong color contrast. Its compact volume gives a straight skirt or slim trousers a deliberate curve. A crisp shirt allows the hardware to become a restrained focal point; a fine cardigan emphasizes the bag’s softer side. This is a particularly coherent choice when an outfit needs one clear, sculptural accessory.

Three details to notice

  • Black Cannage lambskin exterior and lambskin lining.
  • Magnetic double-flap closure with a pale gold-finish CD Lock clasp.
  • Adjustable, removable leather shoulder strap and an interior zip pocket.

How to wear it

Style it with a crisp white shirt, a long charcoal skirt, and low pumps. Use the shoulder strap for daytime, then carry the curved handle to emphasize its sculptural outline.
