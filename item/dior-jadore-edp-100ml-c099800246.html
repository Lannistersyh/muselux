Design notes

J’adore suits a fragrance wardrobe built around flowers rather than a single signature note. The Eau de Parfum brings ylang-ylang, rose and two jasmine varieties into one bouquet, while its rounded amphora bottle supplies the visual drama. This is the 100 ml spray, with the tall gold-toned neck shown in the current official photograph. Consider it alongside silk, a fluid dress or a beautifully cut shirt: clothing that lets a floral scent become part of the overall mood.

Three details to notice

  • Eau de Parfum spray in the 100 ml / 3.4 oz size.
  • Declared floral notes include ylang-ylang and Damascus rose.
  • The composition pairs jasmine grandiflorum with jasmine sambac.

The mood

Sample the Eau de Parfum on skin before choosing a full bottle, then revisit it later in the day. Let the bouquet complement an occasion without assuming a fixed wear time.
