FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

The Smile pendant treats a small gesture as the whole composition. Its gently lifted curve leaves the neckline visually open, with the T-shaped ends supplying just enough definition to hold the eye. There is no central stone competing with the line, so the effect depends on proportion and placement. Think of it as a fine piece of punctuation: it can finish a simple outfit, establish the first line of a necklace arrangement, or sit quietly beside the textures you already like to wear.

Three details to notice

  • A small curved motif with T-shaped ends.
  • 18K yellow gold without gemstones in this variant.
  • An adjustable 16–18 inch chain.

How to wear it

Wear it above the edge of a simple crewneck or against an open neckline. If adding another pendant, choose a visibly longer chain so the two outlines have space to read.
