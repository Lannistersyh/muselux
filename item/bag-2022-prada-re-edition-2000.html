FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A close-fitting shoulder shape gives this little black bag its easy rhythm. The curved top line softens a compact rectangular base, while the nylon surface keeps the visual weight low. A small enamel triangle and hanging tag provide just enough detail against the dark fabric. In an outfit, it works like a quiet punctuation mark: recognisable, compact and easy to repeat. The appeal lies in the balance between a sporty material and a silhouette that still feels deliberate beside tailoring or an evening slip dress.

Three details to notice

  • Re-Nylon body with a woven tape handle
  • Top zipper and enamelled metal triangle emblem
  • Logo-print Re-Nylon lining

How to wear it

Wear it close to the shoulder with a white tank, straight jeans and a slim belt. For evening, let the same compact shape sit beside a fluid black dress and small silver earrings.
