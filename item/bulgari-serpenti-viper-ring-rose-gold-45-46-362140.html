Design notes

Serpenti Viper gives a familiar animal motif a disciplined, geometric rhythm. The ring curves around the finger while its repeated sections let light break into a sequence of small planes. In this plain rose-gold version, the shape carries the expression. It is an interesting choice for a jewelry wardrobe that favors one recognizable detail at a time: turn the hand, change the angle, and the outline becomes the focus. Consider the ring as a compact sculptural element when balancing the volume of sleeves, cuffs and other accessories.

Three details to notice

  • A one-coil Serpenti Viper design in 18K rose gold.
  • Selected reference 362140 corresponds to size 45–46.
  • The official page states that this reference is not resizable.

How to wear it

Let the coil stand alone on one finger, with a plain cuff or a clean sleeve nearby. Compare the rose tone with your usual metals before deciding how much to repeat it.
