FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A circle can be as expressive as a stone. This LOVE pendant makes its impression through the space at its center and the measured rhythm around its edge. From a distance, it reads as a clear golden outline; closer inspection reveals the screws that give the collection its familiar character. That shift in scale is its appeal. The pendant invites a second look while leaving plenty of room for the person wearing it, a useful starting point for a jewelry wardrobe built around strong, simple shapes.

Three details to notice

  • 18K yellow gold with the LOVE screw motif.
  • A circular pendant with a 23.5 mm outer diameter.
  • Supplied on a 42 cm chain.

How to wear it

Frame the circle with an open shirt collar or a plain scoop neckline. K eep other neckwear restrained, then repeat the warm metal with a small pair of hoops or a simple ring.
