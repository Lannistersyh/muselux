FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

This Baguette combines the graphic familiarity of Fendi’s FF pattern with the animated world of Dratini, Dragonair and Dragonite. Microbead embroidery and a fringed edge give the small horizontal silhouette a richly textured finish, while the recognizable clasp and handle preserve the bag’s identity. The result feels unusually joyful for an evening accessory: part character portrait, part surface craft. Its place in the 2024 Year of the Dragon collection gives the design a specific cultural moment beyond the collaboration names.

Three details to notice

  • FF microbead embroidery features Dratini, Dragonair and Dragonite.
  • Fringed Baguette silhouette with detachable handle and shoulder strap.
  • Exact multicolor beaded reference: 8BR600AR1SF1N7O.

How to wear it

Let the embroidery become the focal point with a plain dark dress or a sharp suit. Simple shoes and a small metal earring keep the characters and beadwork visually clear.
