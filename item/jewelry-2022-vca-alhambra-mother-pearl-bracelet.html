FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Five pale quatrefoils turn a fine yellow-gold chain into a repeating pattern around the wrist. Each mother-of-pearl centre catches light a little differently, while the beaded outlines give the motifs a consistent frame. The spaces between them matter as much as the shapes themselves, keeping the bracelet light rather than forming a solid cuff. This photograph shows the identified pre-owned example, measured by its seller at seven inches. Its soft contrast works well beside smooth fabrics, especially when other wristwear is restrained enough to leave the individual motifs visible.

Three details to notice

  • Five white mother-of-pearl motifs framed by 18K yellow gold.
  • Exact pictured pre-owned listing; seller describes 7 in length and excellent condition.
  • Certificate dated November 2023; annual selection refers to the established Alhambra family.

How to wear it

Wear the bracelet against a plain sleeve or alone at a bare wrist. Small gold earrings can repeat the warm contour without adding another strong motif.
