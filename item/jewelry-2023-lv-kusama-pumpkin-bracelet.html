FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A slim Monogram loop becomes the quiet support for two small hanging forms: a bright pumpkin and the familiar LV initials. The warm brown canvas grounds the colour, while the gold-tone fastening and charm fittings supply a clean metallic edge. Unlike a broad cuff, the bracelet relies on movement and small changes in angle to reveal its details. It brings a playful note to an otherwise simple wrist arrangement. Allow a little space around the dangling shapes so the rounded pumpkin outline remains visible, especially when worn with a shirt or lightweight knit.

Three details to notice

  • Brown Monogram-canvas loop with gold-tone hardware.
  • Coloured pumpkin charm paired with LV initials.
  • Exact SS23 catalogue referenceM1006E, released 31 March 2023.

How to wear it

Wear it with a white shirt and a small gold-tone ring. Leave adjacent bracelets off so the pumpkin charm can move freely.
