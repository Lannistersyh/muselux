Design notes

The small Galleria brings the proportions of a structured tote into a compact leather format. Two rounded handles frame the opening, with a triangular Prada signature and hanging keyring giving the front its familiar punctuation. Black calfskin keeps the focus on the bag’s edges and construction. External zip pockets and an optional shoulder strap add practical choices without changing that orderly appearance. This variant is especially appealing when you want a handbag to look composed beside tailoring while still feeling appropriate with a simple shirt and denim.

Three details to notice

  • Calfskin body with leather triangle and metal lettering
  • Detachable, adjustable shoulder strap and removable keyring
  • External zip pockets, nappa lining and leather purse feet

How to wear it

Set it beside a pale shirt, dark trousers and loafers for a concise weekday outfit. Keep other hardware discreet so the triangle and small keyring provide the main points of shine.
