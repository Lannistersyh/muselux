WHY IT BELONGS IN 2023

The year’s attention

The Lock family received its global launch and a ROSÉ-led campaign in January 2023. This plain rose-gold medium bangle represents that year’s documented campaign emphasis, rather than a claimed universal sales ranking.

  • Tiffany · official global Lock launch ↗2 January 2023

FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Without gemstones, the Lock bangle depends entirely on the meeting of two rounded sections. The broader lower arc and slimmer upper curve form one continuous oval, with the hidden fastening keeping the outline visually uninterrupted. Rose gold adds a warm colour that can be read clearly beside white cotton or a pale knit. The design is restrained enough to wear alone but substantial enough to hold a place in a considered wrist arrangement. Begin by checking the medium fit, then decide whether another bracelet adds a useful change of scale or simply hides the shape.

Three details to notice

  • Plain 18K rose gold with a hidden closure.
  • Medium fits wrists up to 6.25 in.
  • Swivelling opening preserves the oval Lock silhouette.

How to wear it

Wear it with a crisp shirt and one simple rose-gold ring. Allow a little space around the cuff so the full oval line can be seen.
