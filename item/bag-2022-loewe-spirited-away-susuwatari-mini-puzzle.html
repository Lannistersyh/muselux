FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Tiny soot sprites make the Puzzle's angular panels feel unexpectedly animated. Against mint-green leather, their dark silhouettes and bright eyes read like little interruptions in a carefully drawn geometric composition. The compact handle and long strap retain the model's recognisable outline, allowing the character work to carry the change in mood. This is a collector's piece with a light touch: the base colour remains easy to place in a wardrobe, while the scattered figures reward a closer look and keep the design from feeling solemn.

Three details to notice

  • Susuwatari characters from Spirited Away decorate the pictured green leather
  • Mini Puzzle body with a top handle and shoulder strap
  • Part of LOEWE's January 2022 Studio Ghibli capsule

How to wear it

Let the mint colour sit against ivory denim, a pale knit and flat sandals. A simple navy coat also gives the small black characters a clean frame without echoing the illustration elsewhere.
