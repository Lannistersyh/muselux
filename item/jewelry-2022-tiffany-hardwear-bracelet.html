FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A chain bracelet becomes especially engaging when its individual links have a clear identity. HardWear uses that repetition to create a steady rhythm around the wrist, with the open spaces keeping the pattern readable. In silver, the changing reflections can feel as graphic as the shape itself. The small-link scale is a good place to study the design: notice where each link meets the next and how the closure joins the sequence. It brings a deliberate detail to clothes that otherwise rely on simple, familiar proportions.

Three details to notice

  • Sterling silver with the HardWear gauge-link pattern.
  • Small link scale; medium wrist size.
  • 7.5 inch overall length, with a listed wrist range of 5.76–6.25 inches.

How to wear it

Roll a shirt cuff just above the bracelet so the link rhythm stays visible. Pair it with a plain ring, or leave that wrist as the only silver accent in the outfit.
