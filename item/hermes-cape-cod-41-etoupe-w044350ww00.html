Design notes

Cape Cod softens a rectangular watch with curved steel outlines and gently drawn numerals. The étoupe strap makes the contrast especially easy to place in a wardrobe of stone, cream and brown. This 41 mm version uses an opaline silvered dial, a date window and a single leather strap, giving the design a more expansive presence than its smaller relatives. Henri d’Origny’s original family dates to 1991; here, the pleasure is still the unusual frame and the warmth of visible leather grain.

Three details to notice

  • Steel case measures 41 mm from lug to lug and 33 mm across.
  • Swiss quartz movement displays hours, minutes, seconds and date.
  • Étoupe Swift calfskin single-tour strap with a steel pin buckle.

How to wear it

Try it beside a camel overshirt or an oatmeal knit, where the taupe strap can echo the clothing. Follow Hermès’ instruction to keep the leather watch away from water.
