FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A brightly set Lock bangle and an apparently eroded bronze padlock make a deliberately contrasting pair. The jewel offers clean white-gold surfaces, green stone accents and a precise curved outline; the sculpture surrounds that clarity with a rough, blue-patinated exterior. Seen together, they turn the act of opening a jewellery container into part of the work. This entry concerns the complete ensemble pictured, including the artwork and its exclusive bangle. It is as much a study in presentation and material contrast as a piece to wear at the wrist.

Three details to notice

  • Exclusive 18K white-gold Lock bangle set with diamonds and tsavorites.
  • Patinated bronze padlock sculpture houses the jewel.
  • Officially documented series of 99 sculpture-and-bangle works.

How to wear it

When wearing the bangle, keep nearby wristwear simple so the green and white stones remain clear. Treat the sculpture as part of the complete collectible ensemble.
