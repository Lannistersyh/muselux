FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Margaux 15 draws attention through proportion and restraint. Its broad base and gently arched handles create an assured outline, while the grained black leather gives the surface interest without a loud emblem. Small buckled side panels interrupt the width just enough to make the shape feel adjustable and alive. It reads as a deliberate everyday object rather than an occasional flourish. Against tailored clothes, the softened structure prevents stiffness; with casual clothing, the same controlled outline provides a useful sense of finish and composure.

Three details to notice

  • Grained calfskin body with belted side gussets
  • Palladium-plated hardware and cotton herringbone lining
  • Two interior card slots and one zipped pocket

How to wear it

Carry it with a long wool coat, a fine knit and relaxed tailored trousers. Keep shoes and jewellery quiet so the bag's scale and grained surface can define the outfit.
