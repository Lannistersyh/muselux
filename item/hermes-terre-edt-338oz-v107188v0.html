Design notes

Terre d’Hermès brings a drier direction to a fragrance collection. The original Eau de Toilette, created by Jean-Claude Ellena in 2006, places grapefruit beside cedar and a flinty mineral note. Its clear rectangular bottle, dark cap and orange detail have a directness that suits the scent’s stated woody character. This entry follows the 3.38 fl oz U.S. bottle. Think of it as a companion to textured fabrics and simple shapes, especially when a floral composition is not the mood you want.

Three details to notice

  • Eau de Toilette in the selected 3.38 fl oz size.
  • Jean-Claude Ellena created the fragrance in 2006.
  • Hermès names grapefruit, cedar and flint as its main notes.

The mood

Try it with a linen shirt, a wool jacket or understated weekend layers. Give a skin sample time to develop before judging its balance; individual wear varies.
