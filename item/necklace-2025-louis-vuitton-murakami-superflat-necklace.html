FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Murakami’s Superflat characters give this layered necklace a sequence of small visual surprises. A colorful LV Hands emblem meets a panda and a cluster of gold-tone Monogram charms, with the different elements distributed along two lines around the neck. Colored enamel adds a deliberately flat, illustrated quality beside the reflective metal. The design works through contrast: a familiar luxury signature is made more playful, and a simple chain becomes a setting for several distinct characters that can be read one by one.

Three details to notice

  • LV Hands emblem in colored enamel.
  • Panda character and gold-tone Monogram charms.
  • Two-line layered effect; exactreferenceM02499.

How to wear it

Let the layered chains sit over a plain tee or an open dark neckline. Keep other charms away from the same area.
