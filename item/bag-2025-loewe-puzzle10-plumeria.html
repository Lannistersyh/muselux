FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Plumeria brings a floral surface to the Puzzle’s precise geometry. Tan calfskin is scattered with hand-applied leather flowers in blue, white and warm neutral tones, creating a three-dimensional rhythm across the small silhouette. The contrast between the flowers and the bag’s angular seams gives the design a particularly tactile character. Reissued for the Puzzle’s tenth anniversary, it revisits the Hawaii-inspired Fall/Winter 2019 Salone design and offers a quieter but equally distinctive counterpart to the capsule’s new Confetti edition.

Three details to notice

  • Small classic-calfskin Puzzle with hand-applied calfskin flowers.
  • Limited tenth-anniversary re-edition of the Hawaii-inspired FW19 Salone design.
  • Tan/Blue reference A510S21XAY-2906.

How to wear it

Pair its tan ground with ecru linen, blue denim or a navy dress. Let one flower color reappear in the clothing, keeping other accessories simple so the applied leather petals remain the focus.
