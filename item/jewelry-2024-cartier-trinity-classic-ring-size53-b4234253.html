FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Trinity makes color and movement inseparable. Three rings share one space, so the composition changes as their positions shift; each gold tone appears, disappears and returns. That quality makes the ring interesting beyond its familiar silhouette. Look at it from above, then from the side, and notice how the gaps contribute as much as the metal. For someone building a mixed-metal wardrobe, it also offers a thoughtful point of connection between pieces that might otherwise feel unrelated, without requiring every accessory to match.

Three details to notice

  • Three intertwined, moving rings.
  • 18K white, rose and yellow gold in one design.
  • Classic model; selected U.S. reference B4234253 is size 53.

How to wear it

Let Trinity connect a yellow-gold necklace with a white-metal watch. Give the neighboring fingers some space, and try its moving structure in person to find the fit you prefer.
