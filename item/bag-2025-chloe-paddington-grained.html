FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

The revived Paddington brings Chloé’s oversized padlock back into a softly slouched daily bag. In Crafty Brown grained buffalo leather, its substantial hardware and C-shaped buckles give the rounded silhouette a familiar, expressive character. The updated design is lighter than the original and uses a single zipper for easier access, while front, back and interior pockets support everyday organization. It is a particularly persuasive match for clothes with movement and texture, where the bag’s relaxed volume feels like part of the outfit rather than a rigid finishing touch.

Three details to notice

  • Grained buffalo leather with an oversized zamak padlock and C-shaped buckles.
  • Updated lighter design with a single zip closure.
  • 36 × 18 × 18 cm; front and back flat pockets plus an inside zipped pocket.

How to wear it

Pair it with flared denim, a fine knit and a short leather jacket, or carry it beside a fluid printed dress. Keep the clothing relaxed enough to match the bag’s rounded volume.
