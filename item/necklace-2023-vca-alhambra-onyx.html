FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Black onyx makes the Alhambra outline unusually crisp. Its dark, flat centre acts almost like an ink shape, while the small gold beads around the edge introduce a warmer, more textured boundary. The fine yellow-gold chain keeps the emphasis on that compact contrast. This particular photograph belongs to a pre-owned sixteen-inch example, so the reference price follows the same item. Against a pale shirt the motif reads as a clear graphic accent; against black, the gold contour and chain become more prominent and the stone feels quieter.

Three details to notice

  • Black onyx quatrefoil with a beaded 18K yellow-gold border.
  • Exact photograph from pre-owned item 0004152.
  • Seller lists 16 in length; model familyVCARA45800.

How to wear it

Wear the dark motif with a pale cotton shirt and a slim gold bracelet. For a quieter effect, place it against a black fine-knit top.
