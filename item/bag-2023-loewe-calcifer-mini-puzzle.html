FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Calcifer creates a small burst of colour inside the Puzzle's dark geometry. His flame-shaped figure sits near one lower corner, with red and yellow crystals carrying the movement upward across the black leather. The rest of the bag remains visually quiet, so the character appears as a vivid interruption rather than an all-over print. This balance makes the piece easy to appreciate both as an illustrated object and as a compact bag. The angular seams supply a composed framework for a character whose whole presence suggests motion.

Three details to notice

  • Black satin-calfskin Mini Puzzle with Calcifer character decoration
  • Crystal details add red and yellow highlights
  • Archived LOEWE link identifies A510U95X64-1100

How to wear it

Set the flame colours against charcoal wool, dark denim or an ivory shirt. Keep other accessories simple; a small red shoe or lip colour can echo the illustration without turning it into a theme.
