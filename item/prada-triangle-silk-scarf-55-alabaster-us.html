Design notes

Prada’s triangle becomes an all-over geometric rhythm on this compact silk twill square. A quiet, pale color combination makes the repeated shapes feel crisp without turning the scarf into a strong block of color. The printed signature sits near an edge, ready to appear or disappear depending on the fold. At 55 centimeters, it introduces less fabric than a larger carré, making it a useful choice for a tidy neck knot or a small accent at the wrist with a simple everyday outfit.

Three details to notice

  • Repeating triangle print with Prada signature
  • Compact 55 × 55 cm square
  • Silk twill; official color reference F0V4C

How to wear it

Roll it into a narrow band and tie it with a small knot above a crewneck knit. The pale geometric print also works beside a white shirt and a clean, unpatterned jacket.
