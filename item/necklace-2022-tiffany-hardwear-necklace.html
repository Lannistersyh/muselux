Design notes

Repeating gauge links give this silver necklace a measured, almost industrial rhythm. Each rounded section turns slightly against its neighbour, so the chain reads as a sequence of highlights and dark spaces rather than a flat line. The small link scale concentrates that idea close to the neckline without relying on a separate pendant. It is a useful way to add weight to a plain outfit while keeping the palette simple. Against a crisp white shirt or a dark crewneck, the linked outline becomes the main point of interest.

Three details to notice

  • Sterling-silver gauge links in the small scale.
  • Continuous linked silhouette without a central pendant.
  • Official description gives an 18-inch length; selector displays 17.8 in.

How to wear it

Place the chain above an open shirt collar or over a smooth dark knit. Let its repeated links take the lead and use compact earrings nearby.
