WHY IT BELONGS IN 2023

The year’s attention

On 13 March 2023, Vogue described the newly launched Andiamo as a bag that could not stay in stock and documented editors wearing it. That dated editorial evidence supports this family-level pick; the pictured lambskin and silver variant is a current reference.

FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

Andiamo gives a familiar woven tote a distinct point of focus. The Intrecciato surface spreads evenly across the body, then gathers the eye toward a knot at the base of the handle. In this current black reference, silver-finish hardware supplies a cool highlight against the leather. The small top handle and sliding long strap create different visual attitudes, from neat hand carry to an easier crossbody line. Its appeal is a balance of repeated texture and one sculptural detail, making the bag feel considered without requiring conspicuous lettering.

Three details to notice

  • Small Intrecciato lambskin body with a sliding crossbody strap
  • Magnetic closure and a compartmented interior with one zipped and two open pockets
  • Silver-finish hardware; 25 × 20 × 10 cm body

How to wear it

Pair the small size with a crisp shirt, straight wool trousers and low leather shoes. A soft trench works well with the woven surface and keeps the knot detail visible near the hand.
