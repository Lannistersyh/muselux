FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A small clover can give a whole neckline its point of focus. This Vintage Alhambra pendant places white mother-of-pearl inside a warm yellow-gold beaded frame, balancing a smooth luminous center with a more detailed edge. Its single motif reads clearly on a fine chain, so the design stays easy to understand even beside textured clothing. Look at how the pale surface changes with the angle of the light: the appeal lies in that gentle shift, framed by a familiar and carefully repeated outline.

Three details to notice

  • Single four-lobed Vintage Alhambra motif.
  • White mother-of-pearl framed by 18K yellow-gold beads.
  • The cited photograph shows a 14.75–16.50 inch adjustable necklace.

How to wear it

Wear it against a dark open neckline, with small gold studs and a clean sleeve. Keep any longer chain clearly below the clover.
