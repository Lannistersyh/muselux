Design notes

The navy Dior Oblique Mitzah is a narrow silk ribbon designed to bring pattern into a small area. One side carries the repeating Oblique motif, while the reverse introduces a contrasting Christian Dior signature band. Pointed ends give a tied knot a precise finish. Its long, slender format makes it especially useful when a full square would feel too voluminous. Worn at the neck or tied to a bag, it creates a small change in rhythm while keeping the rest of an outfit uncomplicated.

Three details to notice

  • Navy Dior Oblique print with pointed ends
  • Contrasting signature jacquard band on the reverse
  • 100% silk twill; made in Italy

How to wear it

Tie a small side knot with an open-neck shirt and let both printed faces show. Alternatively, make one loose bow around a bag handle, leaving the pointed ends clear of the fastening.
