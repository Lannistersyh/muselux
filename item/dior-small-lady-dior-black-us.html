Design notes

The small Lady Dior uses a compact, upright shape to give soft lambskin an architectural presence. Black Cannage quilting creates depth across the body, while pale gold-finish letter charms bring movement to the otherwise disciplined outline. This version includes both a thin leather shoulder strap and a removable chain, offering different ways to carry the same silhouette. It suits someone who enjoys a handbag as a deliberate finishing detail: neat in the hand, expressive beside a simple coat, and distinctive without relying on a large printed logo.

Three details to notice

  • Cannage-stitched lambskin with D.I.O.R. charms
  • Adjustable, detachable leather strap plus removable chain
  • Interior zip pocket; made in Italy

How to wear it

Carry it by the handles with a straight coat and tailored trousers for a clear, compact outline. For dinner, use the chain and let the quilting provide texture against a plain black dress.
