FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

The mini Coco Crush ring offers a close reading of the collection’s surface. Its crossing lines divide the band into small curved sections, letting a familiar textile reference become a piece of jewelry with its own presence. BEIGE GOLD keeps the color conversation subtle, especially beside pale fabrics or other warm metals. A narrow band can still carry a strong idea; here, the interest comes from the way the pattern continues around the finger. Try it alone first, then consider what another ring would add to that rhythm.

Three details to notice

  • The mini version in 18K BEIGE GOLD.
  • A quilted motif formed by crossing incisions.
  • A rounded profile and scalloped edges are collection design details.

How to wear it

Start with one ring and let the quilted surface show against a simple cuff. For a layered look, compare band scales in person and leave enough room for comfortable finger movement.
