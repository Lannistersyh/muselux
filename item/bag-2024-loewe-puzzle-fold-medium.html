FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

The Puzzle Fold translates LOEWE’s interest in geometry into a simple, useful tote. Diagonal panels animate the Warm Desert calfskin, giving an otherwise clean surface a distinct sense of construction. The medium version is designed to collapse flat, an appealing detail for packing and storage, while long handles allow shoulder or hand carry. Its warm color offers an expressive alternative within a neutral wardrobe, and the open silhouette invites the kind of uncomplicated styling that makes a daily bag feel personal.

Three details to notice

  • Shiny calfskin with a suede-lined interior.
  • Geometric panel construction designed to fold completely flat.
  • Medium tote with shoulder or hand carry.

How to wear it

Wear it with an ivory knit and wide trousers to echo the warm leather, or use it as the soft counterpoint to a dark coat. Its flat-folding structure also suits thoughtful travel packing.
