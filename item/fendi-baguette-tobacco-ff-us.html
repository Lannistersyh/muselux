Design notes

The Baguette’s horizontal shape is built around the pleasure of carrying a bag close under the arm. This medium version combines tobacco-brown FF jacquard with dark leather and a gold-finish clasp, creating a warm, graphic surface that reads clearly even in a restrained outfit. The detachable handle and longer strap offer several carrying options. Its appeal lies in the relationship between proportion and pattern: a compact flap silhouette, a generous repeat of the monogram and enough softness to keep the result easy to wear.

Three details to notice

  • Tobacco FF jacquard with gold-finish FF clasp
  • Front flap, magnetic fastening and interior zip pocket
  • Detachable calfskin handle and shoulder strap

How to wear it

Let the warm monogram sit against cream knitwear and dark denim, or carry it with a chocolate jacket for a tonal approach. Use the shorter handle to emphasize the original underarm proportions.
