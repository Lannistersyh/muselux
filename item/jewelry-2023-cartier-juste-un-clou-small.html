WHY IT BELONGS IN 2023

The year’s attention

Rebag’s 2023 Clair Report names the small yellow-gold Juste un Clou bracelet among its most-favorited jewellery styles. The reference shown matches that material and model scale.

  • Rebag ·2023 Clair Report, p.27 ↗2023

FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A single bent nail becomes an open, sweeping line around the wrist. The flat head and pointed end sit close enough to create tension, while the rounded gold body keeps the object visually smooth. In the small model, that contrast feels concentrated and easy to read beside a plain sleeve. The appeal lies in the familiar form being presented at a different scale and with a polished surface. It is a useful choice when an outfit needs one distinctive detail but would be overwhelmed by a broader cuff or multiple dangling charms.

Three details to notice

  • Plain 18K yellow-gold small model.
  • Nail-head and pointed-end silhouette.
  • 2.5 mm width quoted for size 17; model familyB6062617.

How to wear it

Let the nail shape remain visible next to a rolled white sleeve. Use a plain band on the hand to echo the gold without repeating the motif.
