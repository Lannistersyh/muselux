FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

A softly luminous mother-of-pearl centre gives the four-lobed Alhambra motif a gentle presence against its beaded yellow-gold frame. The chain attaches at two points, keeping the outline clear and allowing the small shape to sit as a defined element at the neckline. This photographed pre-owned example has a sixteen-inch chain, according to its seller. Its appeal is the meeting of a familiar contour and a natural surface that changes slightly with the light. It can finish an everyday shirt just as easily as a more open evening neckline.

Three details to notice

  • White mother-of-pearl quatrefoil in a beaded 18K yellow-gold frame.
  • Exact pictured resale referenceVCARA45900, itemN518871.
  • Seller lists a 16 in chain and a 2022 example.

How to wear it

Set the pale motif against navy or charcoal to show the beaded outline. A small yellow-gold stud keeps the overall scale balanced.
