Design notes

CH Forever gives a simple silver band a distinctly graphic identity. Gothic lettering travels across one face, while small cross motifs bring another rhythm to the surface as the ring turns. The appeal lies in this change of emphasis: a compact shape can read as text, ornament or a quiet strip of metal depending on the angle. This resale example is listed in .925 sterling silver, US size 11. The seller describes substantial polishing and regular wear, details that belong to this individual ring and should inform a closer inspection.

Three details to notice

  • Gothic CH Forever lettering and small cross motifs are visible in the two listing photographs.
  • Seller lists .925 sterling silver and US size 11 (20.6 mm).
  • This individual ring is graded Good; heavy polishing and consistent wear are disclosed.

How to wear it

Use the band as a small graphic accent with a white T-shirt, dark denim or an open shirt cuff. Leave a little space between neighboring rings so the lettering remains legible, and check the listed size carefully before planning a stack.
