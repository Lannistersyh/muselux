FORM / MATERIAL / EVERYDAY

Design notes

The 1DR turns a compact shoulder bag into a bold graphic object. Its oversized Oval D plaque stretches across the flap, making the fastening area feel almost architectural against smooth black leather. Rounded lower corners keep that statement from becoming severe, and the short handle echoes the broad curve of the emblem. This version suits a wardrobe built from simple shapes because it supplies the focal point on its own. The effect is direct and playful, with enough polish to move beyond an exclusively denim-led outfit.

Three details to notice

  • Nappa-finish cow-leather body with an enamelled Oval D plaque
  • Magnetic flap with an internal slip pocket and flat compartment
  • Detachable, adjustable strap for crossbody wear

How to wear it

Give the logo room with a fitted T-shirt, wide jeans and pointed boots. A plain column skirt and cropped jacket create a sharper alternative while preserving the bag's graphic impact.
